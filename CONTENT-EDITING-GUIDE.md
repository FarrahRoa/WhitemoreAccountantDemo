# Content editing guide

Editable website content lives in `src/content/`. React components keep layout, animation, and form behaviour; the content modules hold text, links, images, and structured data.

## Main files

- `src/content/siteConfig.js` — company name, logo paths, telephone, email, address, opening hours, social links, navigation, Calendly, maps, and payment URLs.
- `src/content/homeContent.js` — Home hero, image, trust card, introduction, industries, reasons, development panel, fact preview, and CTA.
- `src/content/aboutContent.js` — About page copy and values.
- `src/content/servicesContent.js` — all seven services and shared service-detail data.
- `src/content/factSheetsContent.js` — fact-sheet categories and disclaimer.
- `src/content/formsContent.js` — quote form steps, labels, options, and messages.
- `src/content/footerContent.js` — footer copy and policy links.

## Common edits

Change the telephone number, email, or opening hours in `src/content/siteConfig.js`. Header, footer, and contact details should read from that single source.

Replace the Home hero image by changing `homeContent.hero.image.src`. Store the replacement at `public/assets/images/hero-accounting-consultation.jpg` or update the path to another file under `public/assets/images/`. Keep the `alt` text meaningful.

Edit Home headings and paragraphs in `src/content/homeContent.js`.

Add or edit a service in `src/content/servicesContent.js`. Keep its `slug` stable if existing links must continue to work. The same array drives the navigation dropdown, Home cards, services page, and service detail lookup.

Add a fact-sheet category in `src/content/factSheetsContent.js`. Its slug is generated from the category text by the existing component.

Update social URLs in `siteConfig.socialLinks`. Configure `calendlyUrl`, `googleMapsUrl`, and `onlinePaymentUrl` there when confirmed URLs are available.

## Unconfirmed information

Calendly, Google Maps, online payment, and some footer/social destinations are intentionally blank or `#` until confirmed. Replace those values in `siteConfig.js`; do not edit generated files.

Run `npm run build` to validate changes. Do not edit `dist/`; it is generated by Vite and will be recreated by the build.
